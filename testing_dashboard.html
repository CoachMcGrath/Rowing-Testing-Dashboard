None

Shown beside the name at the top of the app, on the sign-in screen, on printed reports, and as the home-screen icon. A PNG with a transparent background works best. It's shrunk to fit automatically.

Access

The coach password opens the app for everyone on your coaching team. Add an admin password if only the head coach should be able to change Settings: signing in with it gives full access, while the coach password hides Settings. Leave it blank and everyone can change Settings. Forgotten either one? In the spreadsheet, use Rowing app → Show passwords. These are a simple lock, not real security, so keep anything genuinely sensitive out of the spreadsheet.

Spreadsheet

Filled in for you when the app is opened from the spreadsheet (Rowing app → Open dashboard). It ends in /exec. Only change it if you've been told to.

Photo reading licence

The licence code you were sent, e.g. ROW-AB12-CD34. It turns on reading results from PM5 photos. You can add it later; everything else works without it.

Season

The day each season begins, every year. Every test is filed under the season its date falls in: from this date until the day before it next year. When the date comes round again, the app starts a new season by itself, and past seasons stay available in the season tabs.

Squads

One card per squad. Tap a card to open its settings. A squad's name must match the Squad column in your spreadsheet exactly.

Training zones

Training speeds are a percentage of each athlete's 2000m watts, worked out from their PB. Rate is the stroke rate the zone is rowed at. These apply to every squad, and show in the rankings table, individual reports and printed reports.

Shorter test distances

A shorter test is rowed at higher watts than a 2000m, so its result is divided by this factor to get a 2000m equivalent before the zone percentages are applied. A 1000m usually runs about 10% above 2000m watts (1.10).

Colours

Six base colours. Every other shade on screen, in the charts and on printed reports is worked out from these. Pick a colour or type its hex code (e.g. #8A1538) to match your school colours exactly. Changes show straight away. Keep the background and panels dark so text stays readable.

Config block

Paste this over DEFAULT_CFG near the top of the HTML file and the settings travel with the file — no setup needed on the next device.
